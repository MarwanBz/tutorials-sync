A personal learning platform designed to help you master frontend development through tutorials, quizzes, and spaced repetition.

## Purpose

This platform exists to bridge the gap between reading tutorials and truly understanding concepts. By combining:

- **In-depth tutorials** covering React, databases, HTTP, Express, Git, and React Query
- **Interactive quizzes** to test your understanding after each tutorial
- **Progress tracking** to see your improvement over time
- **Spaced repetition** to review and reinforce what you've learned

## Topics Covered

- **React** - Components, hooks, patterns, and best practices
- **Databases** - Design, indexing, and optimization
- **HTTP** - Request/response cycle, caching, and APIs
- **Express** - Building robust backend services
- **Git** - Version control workflows and commands
- **React Query** - Server state management patterns

## Learning Philosophy

**Depth over breadth.** Rather than skimming many topics, focus on deeply understanding core concepts. Each quiz targets specific learning outcomes, and spaced repetition helps cement knowledge over time.

**Practice actively.** Reading isn't enough. Quizzes force active recall and expose gaps in understanding. Track your scores to see progress and identify areas needing review.

**Build real things.** Tutorials connect concepts to actual implementation. Understanding the "why" behind decisions helps you apply knowledge to new problems.

## How It Works

1. **Read** a tutorial on a topic
2. **Practice** with the quiz to test your understanding
3. **Track** your score and see areas to improve
4. **Review** tutorials based on spaced repetition recommendations

Your progress is stored locally, so you can pick up where you left off anytime.
